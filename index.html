Length of Notes and Documents

Set the standard length and detail of your generated notes. You can override this when choosing a template.

Professional Identity

Describe your professional role, client population, and clinical focus so LushNote can generate contextually accurate notes. Include your role, specialty, setting, and the types of presentations you work with.

Example: "I am a consultant psychiatrist working in a public outpatient service in New South Wales, specialising in mood and anxiety disorders in adults. I also conduct comprehensive assessments for ADHD and Autism in adults. My caseload includes complex presentations, comorbid substance use, and patients on community treatment orders."

Treatment Approaches

List your therapeutic modalities and theoretical orientations. LushNote will reflect these in session content, formulations, and management plans.

Example: "I primarily use CBT and ACT. I integrate DBT skills for patients with emotion dysregulation. For trauma presentations I use EMDR. My approach is evidence-based and goal-oriented, with explicit treatment targets reviewed each session."

Document Style

Describe your preferred writing style, terminology, and formatting. Include language conventions, use of client quotes, tone, and any structural preferences.

Example: "Use Australian English. Use professional, neutral language appropriate to psychiatry. Refer to the person as 'the patient'. Use a clear narrative format with logical flow. Incorporate direct patient quotes where they capture key clinical information. Avoid unnecessary jargon and embellishment. Be objective — distinguish between patient self-report, observation, and clinical interpretation."

LushNote is free to use

This tool is built to support clinicians, not to generate profit. You bring your own Google AI (Gemini) API key — your usage is billed directly to your Google account at their standard rates, so there are no shared limits, no queues, and no platform costs on our end.

If usage grows to a point where infrastructure costs become a burden, we may ask for a small voluntary contribution. It would be a last resort, not a goal.. Our goal is to keep access open for every clinician who needs it.
